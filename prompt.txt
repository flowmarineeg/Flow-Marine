## Goal

Convert the system from soft delete to hard delete while keeping the existing business rules as simple as possible.

## Requirements

- Remove soft delete from the system (User is the only exception and should keep its current behavior).
- Convert delete operations to hard delete.
- Do NOT introduce cascade delete.
- Do NOT introduce recursive delete helpers.
- Do NOT introduce delete engines.
- Do NOT introduce shared delete architecture.
- Do NOT introduce generic delete services.
- Do NOT introduce new infrastructure.
- Do NOT redesign the project.

The delete flow must remain extremely simple.

For every entity:

1. When Delete is requested,
2. Check whether the entity is still referenced by related business data.
3. If related data exists:
   - Reject the delete.
   - Return a clear message explaining what still depends on it.
4. If nothing references it:
   - Permanently hard delete the record.

Nothing more.

The administrator is responsible for deleting dependent records manually.

Example:

Service
→ cannot delete while Rounds exist.

Round
→ cannot delete while Voyages exist.

Voyage
→ cannot delete while Bookings exist.

Booking
→ cannot delete while Invoices exist.

Invoice
→ existing financial rules remain exactly as they are.

The system should never automatically delete child records.

No automatic cascade.

No recursive delete.

No transaction-based delete engine.

## Payment Allocation

Do not use proportional payment allocation.

Example:

BookingPaid =
InvoicePaid × (BookingFreight / InvoiceTotal)

This should not be the primary business logic.

In real shipping operations, agents often pay specific bookings, not a proportional percentage across all bookings.

Examples:

- Pay Booking A only.
- Pay Bookings A + B.
- Leave Booking C unpaid.
- Pay one container today and another later.

Therefore, redesign the payment application so that a payment can be applied to specific invoice line items.

The payment record should clearly indicate how much was applied to each booking (invoice line item).

This removes estimation and allows the Agent Financial Account to always show the exact paid and remaining amount for every booking.

Keep the solution as simple as possible.

Do not over-engineer it.

Reuse the current Invoice + Payment architecture.

Only adjust the payment application model to support explicit allocation to invoice line items.

After updating Sprint 033 with this refinement, it is approved for implementation.

SPRINT 008 — BOOKINGS
================================================================================

STATUS: ✅ COMPLETE

GOAL:
  Build the Booking module. Bookings are cargo reservations on a specific
  voyage. Total freight is automatically calculated as Quantity × Freight Rate
  and stored in the database. Booking number is auto-generated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULES: bookings
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                freightRate, totalFreight(server-computed), commodity,
                remarks, status(enum BOOKING_STATUS), isDeleted,
                deletedAt, createdBy, updatedBy, timestamps

# Sprint 053 (V2 S4) - Partner Settlement & Round Closing - implementation log
Status: IMPLEMENTED in the working tree, NOT committed. Plan: sprints/049-sprint.txt section 7.
Flags: accounting (close, reserve, closing entry) + partners (settlement). All default OFF.

DONE
 S1  Round fields       closeStatus (Open|ReadyToClose|Closed), settlementMode, carryForwardMinor, closedAt/By,
                        reopenedAt/By/Reason. Round.status (operational) is NOT touched. Old rounds read Open.
 S2  Close checklist    GET /round-accounting/rounds/:id/checklist. Blockers: open PDAs (overridable with a
                        written reason by round.close.override -> ROUND_HAS_OPEN_PDA), draft V2 invoices,
                        voyages not Completed/Cancelled, vessel without partner shares (when partners is on).
                        Warnings: final expenses not posted, agent statements not settled.
 S3  Settlement engine  partnerSettlement.logic.js (pure) + service. Profit from the Round's ledger lines
                        (Accrual: revenue - expenses; Cash: accrual - receivable outstanding + payable
                        outstanding). Shares by vessel basis points with largest-remainder rounding (always sums
                        to the profit). net = paid directly - collected directly + share. Paid directly =
                        expenses posted to a partner's current account; collected directly = settlements
                        received into a partner's account. Greedy transfers: biggest payer to biggest receiver.
                        Persisted as PartnerSettlement (Draft until close, Final after) + PartnerTransfer.
                        Transfers are posted when marked Paid: DR receiver's 1200 / CR payer's 1200; reversible.
 S4  Reserve            JvReserveMovement. Hold at close (carry forward, round.carry_forward): DR 3000 / CR 2200.
                        Use (reserve.use): DR 2200 / CR 3000, marks the expense fundedFromReserve (post the
                        expense normally afterwards). Refuses above the vessel's reserve balance.
 S5  Close / reopen     Close = one transaction: conditional claim Open -> Closed, re-check the checklist,
                        calculate + finalise the settlement, post the closing entry (each partner's share to
                        their 1200 account, reserve to 2200, balance to 3000), record the Hold. Reopen (admin,
                        reason): reverses the closing entry, deactivates the Hold, settlement back to Draft;
                        refused while partner transfers are Paid (ROUND_HAS_PAID_TRANSFERS) or if the held
                        reserve was already used (RESERVE_ALREADY_USED).
                        assertRoundOpen / roundLock.js is called from: bookings (create/update/delete, both
                        layers, Final Loading + Discharge import), voyages (manual create/update/delete),
                        tracking, expenses (create/update/delete, convert, post, reverse), V1 invoices and
                        payments, V2 invoice draft/issue/cancel/settlement/void/notes, handling charges,
                        statements, Round update/delete. Refusal = 409 ROUND_CLOSED.
                        Bulk netting skips invoices of closed rounds instead of failing.
 S6  Reports            getRoundPnL gained ?basis=accrual|cash and returns `ledgerPnl` (additive, legacy fields
                        untouched, only when accounting is on). JV Partners Settlement Sheet: Excel download.
 S7  Audit              utils/moneyAudit.js (D9-A): before/after/reason, success AND failure, fire-and-forget; the
                        generic auditLogger middleware is untouched. Used by close, close override, reopen,
                        mark-ready, transfer pay/unpay, reserve use. Entries carry _event/_success/_reason/_error
                        inside newValues (the AuditLog action enum is unchanged).
                        Permissions on rounds: close, closeOverride, reopen, carryForward, useReserve.
 S8  Frontend           RoundAccountingPanel on Round Details (checklist + close wizard + reopen, partner
                        settlement table with transfers and Excel sheet, reserve panel); Ledger-basis selector
                        and ledger P&L strip on the Round P&L report; matrix columns for the new actions.

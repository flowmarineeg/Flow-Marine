SPRINT 007 — ROUNDS & AUTOMATIC VOYAGE GENERATION
================================================================================

STATUS: ✅ COMPLETE

GOAL:
  Build the Round module with automatic voyage generation on save.
  When a Round is saved, the system reads the route legs and auto-generates
  one voyage per consecutive leg pair. Voyages are editable (ETD/ETA/ATD/ATA/Status)
  but never manually created.

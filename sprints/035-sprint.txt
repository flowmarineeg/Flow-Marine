# Sprint 035 — Voyage Profitability Report (Round P&L Evolution) + Targeted Expense Evolution
Status: 🟢 REVISED — 7 business decisions confirmed and applied. Phase 0
(Business Validation) is kept in full, with every resolved Business
Question removed and only the genuinely unresolved ones retained. No
code has been written. This is the final, implementation-ready version
of this document.

**Revision 2** — applies 7 confirmed business decisions: (1) this sprint
evolves the existing Round P&L report in place — no new report, endpoint,
or frontend page; (2) Hire Rate is a normal, manually-entered `Expense`
(`ExpenseType = 'Hire Rate'`, voyage-linked, amount = that voyage's day
rate) — no longer blocked; (3) the system operates on a single
operating currency for this sprint — all currency-mixing questions/risks

(per-voyage breakdown, even in Round scope); (5) Other Voyage Cost is
aggregated **by scope only** (one round-wide total, or one voyage total)
— NOT broken out per voyage within Round scope, which is a real
correction to Revision 1's assumption and ripples through Sections
8/12/14, the API shape, and the Summary behavior; (6) Bunker Cost
unchanged — still reserved only; (7) Business Questions trimmed to only
what remains genuinely open. Every section below is updated in place;
structure and numbering are preserved.

This sprint builds directly on two already-completed analyses, treated
here as the architectural foundation and NOT re-derived:
`docs/expense-module-analysis.md` and `docs/rolling-pnl-analysis.md`.
Where this document says "per the Expense analysis" or "per the Rolling
P&L analysis," it is citing a finding already established in those
documents, not re-arguing it.

# SPRINT GOAL

**Evolve the existing Round P&L report — `getRoundPnL` /
`RoundPnlReport.jsx` / `GET /reports/round-pnl` — in place** into the
**Voyage Profitability Report**, extending its current implementation
(not replacing it with a new one) so it works in two scopes — Round and
Voyage — and evolve the Expense module **only** where the validated
business rules require it. The report defines what the Expense module
must support; the Expense module's current shape does not define what
the report is allowed to show.

---

# SCOPE

**In scope:** extending the existing `getRoundPnL`/`RoundPnlReport.jsx`/
`/reports/round-pnl` implementation to cover Revenue (regrouped per
voyage), Voyage Cost, Other Voyage Cost, and a Summary/KPI section, in
both Round and Voyage scope; whatever minimal, justified Expense-side
work is needed to support it. **No new report, no new endpoint, no new
frontend page** — this is an in-place evolution of one existing report,
per the confirmed business decision.

**Out of scope (stated up front, expanded under OUT OF SCOPE below):**
Bunker Cost implementation (reserved only), Expense lifecycle/approval/
soft-delete/audit-trail work (real gaps per the Expense analysis, but
not required by the business rules given for this sprint), and turning
this into a true multi-round time-series "rolling" view (this sprint
delivers voyage-level granularity, which is the larger of the two gaps
identified in the Rolling P&L analysis — the "rolling across time" gap is
not closed by this sprint; see OUT OF SCOPE). Currency conversion/FX is
out of scope by the confirmed single-operating-currency decision, not
because it's unresolved.

## VOYAGE PROFITABILITY BUSINESS SPECIFICATION
### (internal document — single source of truth for this sprint)

### 1. Report Purpose
Answer, for a given scope (one Round, or one Voyage within a Round):
how much revenue did the cargo actually moved (Final Loading bookings)
generate, how much did it cost to move it (port costs + other
operational voyage costs), and what was the resulting profit — in
total, per TEU, and as a margin percentage. This is the direct successor
to Round P&L, gaining the one capability that report never had: a
result broken out per operational voyage, not just per round.

### 2. Revenue Sources
**Layer 2 `Booking` ONLY.** `ManualBooking` (Layer 1) does **not**
participate — this is a deliberate, given business rule for this
report, not an oversight to "fix" toward dual-layer (contrast with
Round P&L, which does combine both layers for its revenue figure; this
report is intentionally narrower, the same way Booking Revenue Details
and Agent Financial Account are intentionally Layer-2-only per their own
stated business purposes). No `Invoice`/`Payment` involvement — this is
a revenue-earned report, not a revenue-collected one.

### 3. Revenue Business Rules
- **Only bookings with `status: 'Final Loading'` participate** —
  identical eligibility rule to Booking Revenue Details (Sprint 034) and
  Agent Financial Account (Sprint 033): cargo that has actually,
  physically moved, not still-pending reservations. Pending and
  Confirmed bookings are excluded entirely, not merely down-weighted.
- **Grouped by operational voyage.** "Operational voyage" = one `Voyage`
  document — each `Voyage` already has its own `polId`/`podId` (a single
  POL→POD leg is exactly what a `Voyage` document already represents in
  this system; no new concept needed, see Data Model Decisions).
- Per operational voyage, display: **POL, POD, Total TEU, Total MT
  (VGM), Total Revenue.**
  - Total TEU: sum of `resolveTEU(containerType.size).teu` across the
    voyage's Final Loading bookings — the exact same reused,
    unmodified `teuResolver.js` call already established in Sprint 034.
  - Total MT (VGM): sum of `Booking.vgm` across the same booking set,
    null treated as 0 — same pattern as Booking Revenue Details' VGM
    column.
  - Total Revenue: sum of `Booking.totalFreight` across the same set.

### 4. Expense Sources
`Expense` (the existing, single collection analyzed in
`docs/expense-module-analysis.md`) — no other source. No new expense
collection is introduced.

### 5. Expense Ownership
Per the Expense analysis: every `Expense` links to exactly one of
`roundId` XOR `voyageId` (never both, never neither) — this rule is
unchanged and is reused as-is. For this report, "ownership" of a cost by
an operational voyage means: `Expense.voyageId` equals that voyage's
`_id`, **or** `Expense.roundId` equals the round's `_id` (a round-level
expense is not tied to one specific voyage, but is still shown in Round
scope — see Section 8).

### 6. Expense Scope
Two business-named groupings of expense, both drawn from the same
`Expense` collection, distinguished by `ExpenseType.category`:
- **Voyage Cost** = expenses whose `ExpenseType.category` is `'Port
  Expense'` (the category already exists, seeded, and already carries
  exactly this meaning in the current system — no new category needed
  for this piece).
- **Other Voyage Cost** = every other expense category linked to the
  voyage/round (`'Operating Expense'`, `'Other'`, or any
  business-created category) — see Section 9 for the itemized
  classification of the specific example line items given for this
  section (Hire Rate, Voyage Duration, Total Hire Cost, Agency Fees,
  Canal Dues, Stevedoring, Miscellaneous).

### 7. Voyage Scope Behavior
When a single Voyage is selected: Revenue shows **only that voyage's**
row; Voyage Cost shows **only the port expenses linked to that voyage**
(via `Expense.voyageId`); Other Voyage Cost shows only that voyage's own
items (see Section 9 for which of these are even voyage-attributable);
Summary totals are computed **for that one voyage only**.

### 8. Round Scope Behavior
When a Round is selected (no specific Voyage):
- **Revenue** shows **one row per operational voyage belonging to that
  round** (every `Voyage` whose `roundId` matches), each with its own
  POL/POD/TEU/MT/Revenue, **plus** a round-wide total row.
- **Voyage Cost** shows **port expenses for every voyage in the round**,
  grouped so the user can see which voyage (and which specific port, per
  Section 9's Voyage Cost detail) each cost belongs to — this is the
  exact per-voyage expense breakdown Round P&L never had (see
  `docs/rolling-pnl-analysis.md` Gap Analysis, "no per-voyage breakdown
  exists anywhere").
- **Other Voyage Cost does NOT get the same per-voyage breakdown**
  (confirmed business decision) — it is shown as **one round-wide
  total only**, not one row per voyage. Internally, an item like Total
  Hire Cost is still computed per voyage (each voyage's own Hire Rate
  Expense × that voyage's own duration — see Section 9) because the
  underlying formula is inherently voyage-specific, but those per-voyage
  results are summed before display in Round scope, never shown
  individually. This is a deliberate difference from how Voyage Cost
  behaves in the same scope.
- **Summary** is computed **once, for the whole round** (Revenue summed
  across every voyage, Voyage Cost summed across every voyage, Other
  Voyage Cost as the single round-wide total above) — there is no
  meaningful "per-voyage Net Profit" while in Round scope, because Other
  Voyage Cost isn't attributed per voyage at that scope; a true
  per-voyage Net Profit is only available by narrowing to Voyage scope
  (Section 7), where Other Voyage Cost is naturally already scoped to
  one voyage.

### 9. Manual vs. Automatic Calculations — Item-by-Item Classification
Per the explicit instruction not to assume every "Other Voyage Cost"
example is an Expense, each is classified below with reasoning.

| Item | Classification | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Hire Rate** | **Manual Expense** (confirmed business decision) | Entered through the existing Expense module exactly like any other expense — `ExpenseType = 'Hire Rate'`, linked via `voyageId` (the amount entered "represents the Hire Rate for that voyage" — a per-voyage day rate, not a round-wide or vessel-wide figure). **No schema change** — this reuses `Expense`/`ExpenseType` precisely as they exist today, the same mechanism as Agency Fees/Canal Dues/Stevedoring below. This was the one item Revision 1 could not resolve from existing architecture; it is now fully unblocked. |
| **Voyage Duration** | **Automatically Calculated** | Derivable today, no schema change: `eta − etd` (planned) or `ata − atd` (actual, once both are recorded) on the existing `Voyage` document. Which pair to prefer (planned vs. actual) remains a small, low-risk open question — see Business Questions. |
| **Total Hire Cost** | **Derived Value** — `Hire Rate × Voyage Duration` | Exactly as given in the business requirements — "the business formula defined by the report." `Hire Rate` is that voyage's own `'Hire Rate'` Expense amount; `Voyage Duration` is Section 9's automatically-calculated value above. Computed per voyage (the formula is inherently voyage-specific); **displayed per voyage only in Voyage scope, summed into one round-wide figure in Round scope** (Section 8). No longer blocked. |
| **Agency Fees** | **Manual Expense** | Fits the existing `Expense` model exactly as-is — `expenseTypeId` (category likely `'Operating Expense'` or a dedicated category), optional `agentId` link (already a field on `Expense`, and unusually well-suited here since this is the one expense type that naturally has an agent). No schema change needed. |
| **Canal Dues** | **Manual Expense** | Same reasoning as Agency Fees — an `ExpenseType` under an existing or new category, no schema change needed. |
| **Stevedoring** | **Manual Expense** | Same reasoning. |
| **Miscellaneous** | **Manual Expense** | Maps directly to the existing `'Other'` category — already seeded, already exists for exactly this purpose. |

**Whether Agency Fees / Canal Dues / Stevedoring warrant their own
dedicated `ExpenseType` categories (vs. all living under the existing
`'Operating Expense'` category, distinguished only by `ExpenseType.name`)
is a data-taxonomy question, not an architecture question** — the
existing `ExpenseCategory` collection already supports adding new
categories with zero schema change (per the Expense analysis, category
creation is already a normal, supported user action). Recommendation
(not a decision): let the business name whichever categories/types it
wants using the existing, already-dynamic category system — no sprint
work is required to "add" these as categories.

### 10. Automatic Calculations (report-wide)
Total TEU, Total MT, Total Revenue (Section 3); Voyage Duration (Section
9); every summary figure in Section 13. All are aggregations or simple
arithmetic over already-recorded data — none require new manual entry
of a calculated result.

### 11. Required Report Sections
1. Scope selector (Round, or Round→Voyage).
2. **Revenue** — one row per operational voyage in scope (POL, POD,
   Total TEU, Total MT, Total Revenue) + a totals row.
3. **Voyage Cost** — port-expense rows, grouped per voyage (Round scope)
   or shown flat (Voyage scope), further identifiable by the specific
   port name (`Expense.port`, the existing free-text field) + a totals
   row.
4. **Other Voyage Cost** — Hire Rate (live, from Expense) / Voyage
   Duration (auto-calculated) / Total Hire Cost (derived, live), Agency
   Fees / Canal Dues / Stevedoring / Miscellaneous (live, from Expense),
   and a **reserved, inert Bunker Cost row** (Section 17) — shown as
   **one scope-wide total** (round-wide, or single-voyage, never broken
   out per voyage within Round scope — Section 8).
5. **Summary** — Section 13.

### 12. Required Totals
Per-section subtotals: Total Revenue and Total Voyage Cost are both
shown **per voyage** (in Round scope) as well as summed across the whole
scope, so a user can see "Voyage 1's revenue was X, its port costs were
Y" without needing the whole round collapsed into one number — the exact
capability Round P&L was missing. **Total Other Voyage Cost is shown
only at the selected scope level** (one round-wide figure, or one
voyage figure), per the confirmed Section 8 behavior — it is not part of
the per-voyage breakdown. All three feed the Summary section's grand
totals (Section 13).

### 13. Profit Calculations
```
Total Revenue      = sum(Revenue section's Total Revenue, across scope)
Total Voyage Cost   = sum(Voyage Cost) + sum(Other Voyage Cost)
                       [Bunker Cost excluded/zero — reserved, Section 17]
Net Profit           = Total Revenue − Total Voyage Cost
Profit per TEU       = Net Profit ÷ Total TEU (across scope)
Profit Margin %      = (Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue) × 100
```
**Currency — confirmed, not an open question.** For Sprint 035 the
system operates on a single operating currency; `Total Revenue`
(`Booking.totalFreight`) and `Total Voyage Cost`/`Other Voyage Cost`
(`Expense.amount`) are combined directly, with no conversion logic. This
supersedes the multi-currency concern `docs/rolling-pnl-analysis.md`'s
Gap Analysis raised against the old Round P&L frontend — that concern
does not carry forward into this sprint's scope. Multi-currency support
may be revisited in a future sprint if required; it is explicitly not
designed here (see OUT OF SCOPE).

### 14. Required KPIs
Total Revenue, Total Voyage Cost, Net Profit, Profit per TEU, Profit
Margin % (Section 13) — computed **once, for the selected scope**
(whole round, or the one selected voyage). Per Section 8's confirmed
behavior, these five KPIs are **not** repeated per individual voyage
while in Round scope (Other Voyage Cost isn't attributed per voyage at
that scope, so a per-voyage Net Profit/Margin would be incomplete/
misleading). A user who wants one specific voyage's own Net Profit
narrows to Voyage scope (Section 7), where all five KPIs are naturally
computed for that voyage alone. Revenue and Voyage Cost alone (without
Other Voyage Cost) remain visible per voyage even in Round scope, per
Section 12 — just not combined into a per-voyage profit figure.

### 15. Required Filters
**Round** (required — the report always operates within one Round) and,
optionally, **Voyage** (narrows to Section 7's behavior). No other
filter is specified in the given business requirements; date-range,
currency, and agent filters are NOT requested and are not assumed here
(unlike the old Round P&L, which had none beyond Round, and unlike
Booking Revenue Details, which has several — this report's filter set is
deliberately whatever the business asked for, no more).

### 16. Dynamic Report Behaviour
**One report, one existing backend endpoint (`/reports/round-pnl`,
extended, not replaced), one existing frontend component
(`RoundPnlReport.jsx`, extended, not replaced) — confirmed business
decision, not two separate reports for Round vs. Voyage, and not a new
report alongside the old one.** Selecting a Voyage narrows the same
data shape the Round view already produces (Round scope's per-voyage
Revenue/Voyage-Cost rows already contain everything a single-voyage view
needs; Voyage scope is a filtered/narrowed view of the same response
shape, not a structurally different one). This mirrors the same "one
function produces the whole payload, scope-parameterized" pattern
already proven in `vessel.service.js#getVesselStats` and
`agent.service.js#getAgentFinancialAccount` — reused here, not
reinvented, and now applied to `getRoundPnL` itself rather than a new
function.

### 17. Future Placeholder for Bunker Cost
**Not implemented in this sprint — position reserved only.** The report's
response shape and UI both carry a `bunkerCost` line item under Other
Voyage Cost, always present, always rendered as an inert "Coming Soon" /
disabled row, and **explicitly excluded from every Summary calculation**
in Section 13 (`Total Voyage Cost`, `Net Profit`, `Profit per TEU`,
`Profit Margin %` are all computed as if Bunker Cost were zero). No field
is added to any model in this sprint for Bunker Cost — the reservation is
a response-shape/UI convention only, so that when a future sprint does
implement it, the summary formulas don't need to change shape again, only
stop treating the value as zero.

## BUSINESS QUESTIONS REQUIRING CONFIRMATION

Revision 1 listed 8 items. Hire Rate's source (former #1), Total Hire
Cost's per-voyage-vs-round scope (former #2), and the Revenue/Expense
currency-combination question (former #4) are now **resolved** by the 7
confirmed business decisions and have been removed from this list
entirely (their resolutions are folded directly into Sections 8/9/13
above). Former #8 (Final Loading eligibility) was already "decided, not
open" in Revision 1 and is removed as noise. **Four items remain
genuinely unresolved:**

1. **Voyage Duration: planned (`eta − etd`) or actual (`ata − atd`)?**
   And what should happen for a voyage where actual dates aren't
   recorded yet (in-progress/future voyages) — fall back to planned, or
   show "—"? This directly affects Total Hire Cost's accuracy (Section
   9), now that Hire Rate itself is resolved.
2. **Should Agency Fees / Canal Dues / Stevedoring be new, dedicated
   `ExpenseType` categories**, or continue to live under the existing
   `'Operating Expense'` category, distinguished only by `ExpenseType.
   name`? Low-risk either way — flagged for a decision, not because the
   architecture can't support either answer.
3. **Does "Voyage Cost" (port expenses) need to be attributable to a
   specific named port per row** (Karachi vs. Sohar vs. Jeddah, as the
   business examples list), relying on `Expense.port`'s existing
   free-text field — given the Expense analysis already flagged that
   field as unvalidated free text prone to spelling drift (e.g. "Jebel
   Ali" vs. "JEBEL ALI")? This sprint does not have to fix that older,
   separately-tracked gap, but this report's per-port breakdown quality
   directly depends on it.
4. **Round-linked (not voyage-specific) `Expense` documents, for Voyage
   Cost specifically** — Section 8 proposes showing them as their own,
   un-attributed line within the per-voyage Voyage Cost breakdown,
   rather than forcing them onto one voyage. (Other Voyage Cost no
   longer has this ambiguity — decision 5 aggregates it by scope
   regardless of link type — so this question is now narrowed to Voyage
   Cost's per-voyage breakdown only.) Confirm this is the wanted
   behavior, versus, e.g., excluding round-linked port expenses from
   Voyage Cost entirely.

# ARCHITECTURE DECISIONS (ADR)

## ADR-1 — Evolve `getRoundPnL` in place; no new report, endpoint, or page
**Confirmed business decision, supersedes Revision 1's "new report"
approach entirely.** `GET /reports/round-pnl` gains an optional
`voyageId` query parameter alongside its existing required `roundId`.
`reports.service.js#getRoundPnL` is extended — not replaced, not
duplicated — to compute the Revenue/Voyage-Cost/Other-Voyage-Cost/
Summary shape described in Phase 0, in addition to (or in place of,
implementation's call) whatever it already returns today. Reuses the
"one function, whole payload, scope-parameterized" pattern from
`vessel.service.js#getVesselStats` / `agent.service.js#
getAgentFinancialAccount` (Spec Section 16) — applied here to an
existing function being grown, not a new one being created.
`RoundPnlReport.jsx` is extended the same way — same file, same route
(`/reports/round-pnl`), same `ReportsHub.jsx` card, with its displayed
title/copy updated to reflect the report's new, broader purpose
("Voyage Profitability" framing) without changing its URL or
underlying identity. This is evolution, not replacement, per the
confirmed decision.

## ADR-2 — Revenue aggregation reuses Sprint 034's Booking Revenue
Details mechanics, regrouped by Voyage instead of by pricing-group
Same fetch-then-group-in-JS shape, same `resolveTEU()` reuse (unmodified,
per Decision 8's precedent in `sprints/034-sprint.txt`), same VGM-sum
pattern. The only structural difference is the group key: one `Voyage`
document instead of a six-part pricing-group key. `teuResolver.js` is
NOT touched, same as every prior sprint that has used it.

## ADR-3 — Voyage Cost and Other Voyage Cost both reuse the existing
`Expense` model, unmodified, for every item confirmed as a Manual
Expense in Spec Section 9
Per the Expense analysis, `Expense`'s `roundId`/`voyageId` XOR link, its
`expenseTypeId` → `category` relationship, and its `port` free-text
field already fully satisfy Voyage Cost and the Manual-Expense items
under Other Voyage Cost. **No schema change to `Expense` or
`ExpenseType` is justified by this sprint's validated rules.**

## ADR-4 — Round-scoped expense resolution queries `Voyage.find({
roundId })` directly, NOT `Round.voyageIds`
The existing Round P&L trusts the denormalized `Round.voyageIds` array
without verification (`docs/rolling-pnl-analysis.md`, "Critical
dependency" / Gap Analysis row on `Round.voyageIds` integrity). This
report instead resolves a round's voyages via a direct `Voyage.find({
roundId, ... })` query — the same convention already used elsewhere in
the codebase (e.g. `BookingForm.jsx`'s Round→Voyage cascading filter,
`bookingImport.service.js`'s Round-scoped voyage resolution) — rather
than reusing Round P&L's own weaker pattern. This is "reuse existing
architecture" applied to the *safer* of two existing patterns already
in the codebase, not a new one.

## ADR-5 — Expense category is the sole mechanism distinguishing Voyage
Cost from Other Voyage Cost
`ExpenseType.category === 'Port Expense'` → Voyage Cost;
everything else → Other Voyage Cost. Reuses the existing, already-dynamic
`ExpenseCategory` system exactly as-is (Spec Section 6).

## ADR-6 — Hire Rate is a normal Expense; Total Hire Cost is a live,
computed value (no longer blocked)
**Confirmed business decision, supersedes Revision 1's "BLOCKED" status
entirely.** Hire Rate is created through the existing Expense
create/edit form exactly like Agency Fees/Canal Dues/Stevedoring — a new
`ExpenseType` named `'Hire Rate'`, `Expense.voyageId` set to the specific
voyage it applies to, `Expense.amount` holding that voyage's day rate.
No schema change to `Expense`/`ExpenseType` (same conclusion as ADR-3,
now extended to cover Hire Rate too). Total Hire Cost = that voyage's
`'Hire Rate'` Expense amount × that voyage's Voyage Duration (Spec
Section 9) — computed per voyage, displayed per voyage only in Voyage
scope, summed into the round-wide Other Voyage Cost total in Round scope
(Spec Section 8). The one remaining open point is Voyage Duration's
exact formula (planned vs. actual dates — Business Question 1); Hire
Rate's own source is fully resolved.

## ADR-7 — Bunker Cost is a reserved, always-present, always-inert line
item; no model change
Per Spec Section 17. The response object always includes a `bunkerCost:
null` (or equivalent explicit "not yet implemented" marker) field under
Other Voyage Cost; the frontend always renders its row, disabled,
labeled "Coming Soon"; every Summary formula treats it as 0/excluded.

## ADR-8 — No Expense lifecycle/approval/soft-delete/audit-trail changes
in this sprint
Real, documented gaps (`docs/expense-module-analysis.md`) — but nothing
in this sprint's validated business rules requires them. Per the
instruction to redesign the Expense module "only where necessary," they
are explicitly deferred, not silently dropped — see OUT OF SCOPE.

# DATA MODEL DECISIONS

- **No changes to `Booking`, `ManualBooking`, `Invoice`, `Payment`,
  `Round`, `Voyage`, `Vessel`, `Service`, `Port`.**
- **No changes to `Expense` or `ExpenseType`** for anything this sprint's
  validated rules require — Voyage Cost, Hire Rate, Agency Fees, Canal
  Dues, Stevedoring, and Miscellaneous **all** fit the current schema
  exactly, per ADR-3/ADR-6. Hire Rate's resolution (Revision 2) confirms
  this extends to every item in Other Voyage Cost that is a real
  transaction — nothing in Section 9's classification needs a new field.
- **No new collection.** The report is a read-only extension of an
  existing aggregate function (`getRoundPnL`), the same architectural
  shape as every other report in the system.
- **Bunker Cost: no field added anywhere** (ADR-7) — reserved at the
  report response-shape level only, not the data-model level.
- **No currency-related model change** — the single-operating-currency
  decision requires no schema work; `Expense.currency` and the absence
  of a currency field on `Booking` are both left exactly as they are.

# BACKEND TASKS

# UI REQUIREMENTS

- Scope indicator always visible (which Round, and — if narrowed —
  which Voyage) so the user never has to guess what they're looking at,
  the same "state your scope before showing numbers" discipline already
  used by every report in this module.
- Per-voyage rows (Revenue, Voyage Cost) in Round scope must be visually
  distinguishable from the round-wide totals row (matches the existing
  GRAND TOTAL row convention already used in `RevenueReport.jsx`/
  `VoyagePerformanceReport.jsx`).
- Other Voyage Cost, being scope-wide only (never per-voyage in Round
  scope — Spec Section 8), must be visually presented as a single
  section-level block, not implied to be broken out per voyage the way
  Voyage Cost is — to avoid the user misreading it as per-voyage detail
  that doesn't exist at that scope.
- The Bunker Cost row must be visually distinct from live data
  (disabled/greyed styling), never silently shown as "0" in a way that
  could be misread as "confirmed zero cost" rather than "not yet
  available." Hire Rate/Voyage Duration/Total Hire Cost are now live
  data (ADR-6) and are styled the same as every other live figure — only
  Bunker Cost remains in the inert/placeholder treatment.

# ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

1. Revenue is computed exclusively from Layer 2 `Booking` documents with
   `status: 'Final Loading'` — confirmed by code review that
   `ManualBooking` is never queried by this report's revenue path.
2. Each Revenue row represents exactly one `Voyage` document (one
   operational voyage), never merged across voyages, never split within
   one.
3. Selecting a Round with no Voyage shows every voyage belonging to that
   round plus round-wide totals; selecting a Voyage narrows every
   section to that one voyage plus its own totals — verified for both
   Revenue and Voyage Cost sections.
4. TEU and VGM figures reuse `teuResolver.js#resolveTEU()`/the existing
   VGM-sum pattern unmodified — confirmed by code review that
   `teuResolver.js` itself was not changed.
5. Voyage Cost rows are grouped per voyage (Round scope) and further per
   port, sourced from `Expense.port` — confirmed no schema change was
   made to `Expense` to support this.
6. Agency Fees, Canal Dues, Stevedoring, and Miscellaneous are read from
   existing `Expense`/`ExpenseType` records with no schema change.
7. Hire Rate is read from a manually-created `Expense`
   (`ExpenseType = 'Hire Rate'`, voyage-linked); Total Hire Cost = that
   Expense's amount × the voyage's computed duration; both are live,
   included in every Summary calculation — no schema change to
   `Expense`/`ExpenseType`.
8. **Other Voyage Cost (including Total Hire Cost) is shown as one
   scope-wide total only — never broken out per individual voyage while
   in Round scope** — confirmed by code review that no per-voyage
   Other-Voyage-Cost rows are produced in Round scope.
9. Bunker Cost is always present in the response and UI, always inert,
   and always excluded from Net Profit/Profit-per-TEU/Profit-Margin —
   confirmed no calculation anywhere includes it.
10. Net Profit = Total Revenue − Total Voyage Cost (Voyage Cost + Other
    Voyage Cost, Bunker excluded); Profit per TEU = Net Profit ÷ Total
    TEU; Profit Margin % = Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue × 100 — computed
    **once for the selected scope** (whole round, or the one selected
    voyage) — never as a per-individual-voyage figure while in Round
    scope (Spec Section 14).
11. No change to `capacity.service.js`, `teuResolver.js`'s own rule set,
    `booking.model.js`, `manualBooking.model.js`, `expense.model.js`,
    `expenseType.model.js`, `invoice.model.js`, or `payment.model.js`.
12. `GET /reports/round-pnl` remains a single, existing endpoint — no
    new route was added; the report's pre-existing round/service/vessel/
    expense fields continue to be returned alongside the new
    voyage-level fields.

# RISKS

- **`Expense.port` data quality** (free text, no canonical reference) —
  Voyage Cost's per-port breakdown is only as clean as what's already
  been typed into that field historically; inconsistent naming will
  silently fragment what should be one port into several rows (Business
  Question 3).
- **A `'Hire Rate'` Expense entered against the wrong voyage, or with a
  lump-sum amount instead of a day rate, silently produces a wrong Total
  Hire Cost** — since Hire Rate is now just a normal, unconstrained
  `Expense` amount (no validation distinguishes "this is a day rate" from
  any other expense amount), a data-entry mistake here isn't caught by
  the system, only by whoever reviews the resulting Total Hire Cost
  figure. Not a blocker, but a real data-quality risk worth naming since
  Sprint 034 hire-rate work is otherwise fully unblocked.
- **Round-level (un-attributed) `Expense` documents inside Voyage Cost's
  per-voyage breakdown** could read as confusing to end users if not
  clearly labeled as "belongs to the round as a whole, not any single
  voyage" (Business Question 4) — a UI/copy risk, not a data-correctness
  one.
- **Users may expect Other Voyage Cost to be broken out per voyage in
  Round scope, the same way Voyage Cost is** — the two sections
  deliberately behave differently (Spec Section 8), which is correct per
  the confirmed business decision but is also the one place in this
  report where two sibling sections don't share the same drill-down
  depth; worth deliberate UI/copy attention so it reads as intentional,
  not inconsistent.
- **Single-operating-currency assumption (Decision 3) is a scoped-down
  simplification, not a permanent architectural conclusion** — if the
  business already operates across multiple real currencies today, this
  sprint's Net Profit figures will be numerically wrong the moment that's
  true, silently. Accepted as an explicit, confirmed trade-off for this
  sprint, not a hidden risk — flagged here so it isn't forgotten.

  this sprint closes the **voyage-level granularity** gap identified in
  `docs/rolling-pnl-analysis.md`; it does not add a rolling/trend/
  multi-period view. "Rolling P&L," in the full sense implied by that
  name, remains only partially delivered after this sprint.
- Any change to `Port`/Location master data (e.g. making `Expense.port`
  a real reference) — Business Question 3 flags the dependency but does
  not authorize solving it here.
